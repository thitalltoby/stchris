## E. Known behaviour worth knowing

- Browser-test logs contain "destination stream closed early" lines from the web server. They appear when a test navigates away while a response is still streaming. No test failed because of them and no cause inside the application was found.
- A list is not an order. Sending it creates a request; nothing is reserved, priced or promised until a quotation is accepted.
- A product removed from the catalogue stays on a customer's list as a described item, and past requests and quotations keep their own wording and SKU.
- A customer who registers with a different email address from the one used on a guest request will not see that request. Staff can still see and serve it.
- A visitor's list is kept for 90 days in a server-side record found by a random, hashed token in an `httpOnly` cookie. It is attached to their account when they sign in or register.
